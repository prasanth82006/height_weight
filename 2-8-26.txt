### 2. PINHOLE CAMERA MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION
Replace fixed pixel scaling with true trigonometric pinhole projection:
1. Focal Length (f_y in pixels):
   f_y = (Image_Height / 2) / tan(radians(VFOV) / 2)   [Default VFOV = 55°]
2. Angle Subtended relative to Optical Center:
   dy_top = (Image_Height / 2) - Y_top
   dy_bottom = (Image_Height / 2) - Y_bottom
   alpha_top = arctan(dy_top / f_y)
   alpha_bottom = arctan(dy_bottom / f_y)
3. Physical Height Calculation:
   H_top = Subject_Distance * tan(alpha_top)
   H_bottom = Subject_Distance * tan(alpha_bottom)
   Estimated_Height = H_top - H_bottom
